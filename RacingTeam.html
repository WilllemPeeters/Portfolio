Project

Racing Team

Creating a race car for competitive racing

For half a semester i have worked together with the Delft Formula Cruisers team on the year long development of an electric racing car.

Part of "The Formula Cruisers": an 0fficial student racing team from The Hague University of Applied Sciences in Delft that designs and builds high-performance electric race cars for international competitions.

I personally designed the steering system and the anti roll bar and together with my department designed the suspension system for the car.

I also worked on smaller project like calculation models and plastic housing for the electronics.
