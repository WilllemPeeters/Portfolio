Internship

Lely Feeding Team

For half a semester I have done an internship at Lely, a global multinational in agriculture and the dairy industry.

Unfortunately, I can't show what I have worked on personally, but I was mainly part of the feeding team. Working on an autonomous robot that could load, measure, prepare and distribute cattle food. Providing the ultimate feeding quality and frequency while taking work away from the farmer.

The current version of this robot is called the Lely Vector

Internship

I'm really glad I did my first internship at Lely, because I got the chance to work with lots of people from lots of different fields. It was the first time I really worked as a product designer in a big company and the first time I spoke regularly with customers and manufacturers.

Prototyping

But I mainly chose Lely because I wanted to work on my prototyping skills. This was something that I had not done so much in my first I.p.o. years and Lely was a great place to catch up on that way of designing.​​​​​​
