Project

Sustainable Packaging

A design research project to find a sustainable and realistic replacement for EPS packaging.

The problem

Within the next 3-5 years a ban and/or tax will be put on EPS packaging. Because of this, Smartwares needed an alternative for their packaging methodes.  The project consisted mainly of researching an testing materials, visiting packaging factories, improving Smartwares packaging efficiency and test sustainability with LCA's and consulting experts. 

Doing a project like this really shows you why paper is the future of packaging. We discovered how versatile paper was with its packaging properties. Depending on how you cut, fold or crumple it, paper can be used for shock absorbing, holding stuff in place, protection and basically everything you can ask from a packaging methode.

Its also way better for the planet ofcourse. Paper can be recycled 7 times before it has the be thrown away and even after disposal is still biodegradable, unlike plastics which are used once and wont disappear for a couple million years.
