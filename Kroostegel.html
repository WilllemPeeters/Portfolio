Graduation Project

De Kroostegel

In Januari 2023 I started my graduation internship at Flip The City. I worked on a product called the "Kroostegel". A sustainable and easy to use alternative for potting soil. Made fully from duckweed.

The Project

Streets and surfaces in cities are almost always made of stone or concrete. Which is giving cities problems with their water management, heat management, and biodiversity.

Initiatives like  "geveltuintjes" or "tegelwippen" try to solve these issues by replacing parts of streets/pavement with small gardens. To create small patches of nature where heat and water can escape, and plants can grow.

The problem with this, is potting soil. Potting soil has a very high carbon footprint. High enough that if you plant a plant in potting soil, the CO2 the plant absorbs during its lifetime. Will not compensate for the CO2 emitted by the production of the soil the plant is planted in.

This is where duckweed comes in. When duckweed grows it absorbs nitrogen and other nutrients from the water, which it can provide to plants when scooped up and dried. And because it's a waste product form most municipalities. It does not have a high carbon footprint.

Material Science

The project was heavily focussed on material research and required many hours of experimenting with the duckweed. Trying different proportions, chemicals, order of operations, etc. 

Prototyping

The end product was a solid but organic-looking tile of compressed duckweed. It was nonsticky, easy to place, and looked nice in between plants or soil.

When water is poured over it the duckweed particles would slowly fall off and seep into the ground, releasing the duckweed nutrients in the soil. Plants can be placed next to, on top of, or even into the tile. For municipalities, seeds were mixed into the tiles. So plants would grow a couple of weeks after placement.

Besides creating the product the project focussed a lot on the production plan.  Lots of experimenting with the duckweed to create the perfect consistency and material for consumers, while having a realistic production plan for the company's relatively small production series.

And working out every step in this production series. Creating moulds, testing methods, creating packaging, etc.
