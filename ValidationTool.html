Project

Validation Tool

Designing a multi-use laboratory tool for taking measurements and calibrating machines. The product had to be handheld, easy to use, laboratory proof and be able to measure: airflow, airpressure, electric properties and temperature.

The project

The project focused heavily on the inside of the product with great detail on the positioning, placing and working of every part and every way of measurement. A custom UI was also developed. 

Production

The housing was going to be injection molded and was carefully tested for every step of the production process. The entire assembly process was also thought out and documented in a step by step instruction manual.
