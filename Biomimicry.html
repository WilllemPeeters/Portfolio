Biomimicry

Reducing noise pollution from trains

Definitely my favorite project I have worked on was an assignment using the biomimicry design methodology. Biomimicry means learning from nature and using techniques from nature to solve your own design problems.

The project

The project I have worked on was assigned by ProRail as part of there noise reduction program. The goal of the project was creating a wall like product that could be placed near or next to traintracks to reduce noise pollution by trains.

Involving Nature

The back and top of the product are covered with a green roof which make the wall nice to look at but are mainly for disrupting the natural ecosystems as less as possible and making the product involved with its environment.
