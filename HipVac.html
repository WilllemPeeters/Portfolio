Project

HipVac

In the third year of IPO, I had the possibility to specialize in either packaging or product design. I chose product design and spend half a semester working for a company on a concept for a portable vacuum.

*Because of arrangements with the client some detials on this page (including colour), have been modified.

The project

The concept was ment to be worn around a person working in a workshop and collect sawdust using a vacuum. The concept consisted of a tube which could be used alone or be attached to a saw, sander, etc. And a vacuum engine which collected all the sawdust in a transparant collection tray.

A lot of time was spend on the ergonomics of the product to make sure the product was comfortable to wear and had a pleasant weight distribution. The collection tray was also easy to remove and had te be removed at an angle so no sawdust could fall in the product during removal
